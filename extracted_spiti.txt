ABOUT

Let us explain
your journey 
Day - 0 { journey begins }
Start your journey from your hometown.
• If travelling by train, the journey will start a
day prior.
• If travelling by flight, you will start from
Delhi on the same day.

Day 1
Delhi - Shimla
Begin your journey with an overnight drive from
Delhi/Chandigarh to Shimla in a comfortable
vehicle/Volvo. 
Enjoy the gradual transition from plains to hills
as you head towards Himachal Pradesh.

 
Day 2 
SHIMLA-SANGLA
Arrive in Shimla and begin yourdrive
towards Sangla. 
Travel along thescenic Sutlej River,
passing through beautiful valleys, apple
orchards, and small Himalayan villages. 
Reach Sangla by evening and check in to
your hotel/homestay. 
Spend the evening at leisure exploring the
surroundings or relaxing amidst nature.
Stay - sangla

 Day 3 
SANGLA-CHITKUL - KALPA
After breakfast, visit Chitkul, the last inhabited
village near the Indo-Tibet border, located
along the Baspa River. 
Enjoy breathtaking mountain views and serene
landscapes. Later, drive to Kalpa, known for
its stunning views of the Kinner Kailash range.
Witness a beautiful sunset over the snow-
capped peaks.
Stay: Kalpa

Day 4 
KALPA - TABO
Postbreakfast, begin your journey towards Tabo
via the picturesque Nako village. Stop at Nako
Lake and explore the quiet surroundings. 
Continue driving into the Spiti region, experiencing
a shift in landscape to barren mountains and
rugged terrain. 
Visit the ancient Tabo Monastery, a UNESCO-
listed site known as the “Ajanta of the Himalayas.” 
Stay: Tabo

 Day 5 
TABO - DHANKAR - KAZA
After breakfast, head towards Dhankar
Monastery, dramatically located on a
cliff overlooking the confluence of
Spiti and Pin Rivers. 
You can opt for a short trek to Dhankar
Lake for panoramic views. 
Later, drive to Kaza, the main town of
Spiti Valley. Check in and relax. 
Stay: Kaza

 Day 6
 
KAZA  LOCAL
Today,explore some of thehighest
inhabited villages in the world. 
Visit Key Monastery, the largest monastery
in Spiti, followed by Kibber Village. 
Continue to Hikkim (world’s highest post
office), Komic (one of the highest
motorable villages), and Langza (famous for
Buddha statue and fossils). 
Return to Kaza by evening.
Stay: Kaza

 Day 7 
KAZA - CHANDRATAL
After breakfast, drive towards Chandratal
Lake via Kunzum Pass. Enjoy stunning views of
glaciers and high-altitude landscapes along
the way. 
Reach Chandratal and take a short walk to the
crescent-shaped lake, known for its crystal-
clear waters and changing colors. 
Enjoy dinner and an overnight stay in camps
under a sky full of stars.
Stay: Chandratal Camps

 Day 8 
CHANDRATAL - MANALI
Afterearly breakfast, beginyour journey
to Manali via Batal and Gramphu. Cross
challenging terrains and experience one
of the most adventurous routes in
Himachal. 
Pass through Rohtang Pass (subject to
availability) and descend into the lush
green valleys of Manali. 
Check in to your hotel and relax.
Stay: Manal

 Day 9 
MANALI LOCAL & DEPARTURE
Afterbreakfast, exploreManali’spopular
attractions including Hadimba Devi Temple, 
Vashisht Temple (hot springs), and Mall Road for
shopping and cafés. In the evening, board your
Volvo bus for an overnight journey back to Delhi.

 Day 10 
ARRIVAL IN DELHI
Arrival in Delhi
Continue your journey to your hometown
• Flight travellers reach the same day
• Train travellers may reach the next day (Day 10)
